Frequently asked questions

What is RawReel?

RawReel is a free video site. Our motto is "Your channel's backup. Classics that stay free." It does two things:

  • Backs up your channel. We keep a copy of your own videos, so if YouTube removes a video or your channel, it stays up here. RawReel runs alongside YouTube. It isn't a replacement, so keep posting there as usual.
  • Shows free classics. Old films and cartoons that anyone is allowed to share.

To be upfront: the site is very rough and early. Expect rough edges.

How do I back up my channel today?

Right now it's a few manual steps, not one click (see https://rawreel.github.io/import.html):

  1. Use Google Takeout to export your YouTube videos and their details (a videos.csv file).
  2. On the import page, pick your videos.csv (and, if you want, your video files). They're read in your browser only. We never receive them.
  3. Put each video on your own free Internet Archive account, paste its Archive ID next to the title, and press Submit. That opens an email to us with the list, and we add the videos to your backup.

What comes over: video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnails. What doesn't: views, likes, comments and subscribers. Only back up videos you made or have the rights to.

Where are my videos stored?

While we're small, videos are stored with outside hosts: Internet Archive, plus a PeerTube server as a backup. Their terms apply too, and if a host removes something, we'll tell you. You keep ownership of your videos and can download them anytime. Please keep your own copies as well.

What gets removed, and what doesn't?

We never remove a video for its viewpoint. Unpopular, controversial or political opinions stay up.

Only these come down, under our written rules:

  1. Illegal content (for example, child exploitation, real threats of violence, or pirated material)
  2. Doxxing or targeted harassment
  3. Adult content that isn't labeled (labeled adult content is allowed behind an 18+ check)
  4. Spam or scams
  5. Anything under a valid DMCA copyright notice

Every removal is public. It shows the rule number and the reason on the video's page. You can appeal any removal at RR.Support.Agent@gmail.com. There are no shadowbans. Three uncountered copyright strikes close an account.

What does "classics" mean?

Films that are free for anyone to share:

  • US public domain films, such as silent films and cartoons from 1930 or earlier (Chaplin, Keaton and others)
  • US government works, such as NASA mission footage
  • Open movies under CC BY, which we show with the required credit

Some films play silent because the music added to later copies isn't cleared for sharing. Free classics: https://rawreel.github.io/classics.html

What is RawReplay?

RawReplay (https://rawreel.github.io/replay.html) is RawReel's music section: a free place for independent artists to share music they made themselves. It's still a prototype. The tracks shown are placeholders, and the submit form doesn't send anything yet. When it opens, it will take original music only (no covers, leaks or uncleared samples), artists keep their rights, and every submission will be reviewed.

What's planned?

These features aren't live yet. They have no dates, and we can't promise them:

  • Our own server: a RawReel PeerTube video server.
  • Direct uploads to that server.

Until then, use the Google Takeout steps above.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads right now. If we add them, they'll be short and skippable after 5 seconds, and we'll update our terms first.

Back up your channel

RawReel keeps a copy of your videos with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails. If YouTube removes a video or your channel, it stays up here. It's free, and your files are read in your browser only. Only videos you own: backing up someone else's channel or re-uploads isn't allowed and will be taken down under the rules.

1

Export with Google Takeout

Go to takeout.google.com and sign in with the Google account that owns the channel. If your channel is a Brand Account, switch to that account first, or the export will come back without your videos. Click Deselect all, then tick only YouTube and YouTube Music. Under All YouTube data included, deselect all, then tick videos and video metadata.

2

Create and download the export

Choose Export once, .zip and the largest file size, then Create export. Big channels can take hours or even days; Google emails you when it's ready. Download every part and unzip them into one folder. Inside Takeout/YouTube and YouTube Music/ you'll find videos/ (your video files) and video metadata/videos.csv (one row per video). Large channels get several CSV files; pick them all.

3

Choose your files and submit

On the import page, pick your videos.csv and, if you want, your video files so they can be matched to titles (only the names are read). Put each ticked video on your free Internet Archive account, paste its Archive ID next to it and press Submit. That opens your email app with each title and Archive ID filled in, addressed to RR.Support.Agent@gmail.com, and we add them to your backup.

Back up your channel

What comes over?

Your video files, titles and descriptions. Thumbnails too: Takeout doesn't include them, so we copy your public YouTube thumbnail by video ID. Views, likes, comments and subscribers can't be moved, so you start fresh here.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your Takeout files are read by your browser on your device; RawReel never receives the CSV or video files. Only the titles and IDs you email us are sent, when you press Submit.

Can I back up someone else's videos?

No. Copied videos are taken down when the owner reports them, per the rules, and the removal is noted publicly.

Do I have to leave YouTube?

No. RawReel is a backup, not a replacement. Keep posting on YouTube; your channel there stays exactly as it is.

Submit a video

Backing up a whole channel? Use Back up your channel instead. For a single video, the team reviews every submission (rights and file) before it appears here.

1

Put your video online

On YouTube, or on Internet Archive with a free Archive account. Only videos you made, have the rights to, or that are public domain or Creative Commons. If it's on Internet Archive, the link is archive.org/details/your-video-id.

2

Fill in the details

Title, channel, a short description and the link.

3

Send it for review

Press Send for review. This opens your email app with everything filled in; just press Send. We check the rights and the file, then add it. If it can't go up, we'll email you why. We'll reply to the email you send from. No email app opened? Send the title, channel, link and a line saying you hold the rights to RR.Support.Agent@gmail.com.

Rules, removals and appeals

Draft rules for discussion, not final

If a rule isn't written here, we don't enforce it. Same rules for every creator.

1

Nothing illegal

No content that breaks US law, like child exploitation, real threats of violence, or pirated material that gets a valid legal takedown.

2

No doxxing or targeted harassment

Don't post people's private info or organize attacks on a specific person.

3

Label adult content

Graphic or adult videos are allowed if they're marked, so viewers can choose to see them.

4

No spam or scams

No fake giveaways, phishing links, or mass-uploaded junk.

5

Opinions are not a violation

Unpopular, controversial, or political views are never removed just for being controversial.

✓

Every action is public, and there are no shadowbans

When a video is restricted or removed, the rule number and the reason are posted on the video's page and in the creator's moderation log. Videos are never secretly hidden from search or feeds. If we limit something, we say so.

✓

You can appeal, and you can leave

Every action can be appealed at RR.Support.Agent@gmail.com, and creators can download all of their videos at any time.

RawReplay music

RawReplay is RawReel's music section, and it's still a prototype. The tracks shown are placeholders (CC0 test tracks from the Internet Archive), and music submissions aren't open yet: the share form doesn't send anything, so please don't use it to share your music yet. The short version of the rules: only post music you made and have the right to share.

1

Own it, no leaks, no other people's music

You must own, or have permission for, everything in your upload: the song, the recording, the lyrics and the cover art. No unreleased songs by other artists, snippets or early copies, and no full songs, rips, remasters, sped-up, slowed or nightcore edits of someone else's tracks.

2

Samples, covers, AI and beats

Samples need clearance or must come from a royalty-free pack whose license allows it. Covers need the songwriter's permission or a cover license, so leave them out for now. If you used a beat or AI tool, its license has to allow you to share the result publicly.

3

Open license only

Every track is shared under CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, so listeners can play it and share it with credit. NonCommercial (NC) and NoDerivatives (ND) licenses can't be accepted. Everything in the RawReel rules also applies, and we never remove a song for its opinion.

©

Copyright notices and strikes

If a rights owner sends a valid copyright notice, we take the track down and tell you why. If you believe it was a mistake, you can send a counter-notice; if the other side doesn't take legal action within 10 to 14 business days, we may put the track back. Sending a false notice or counter-notice can make you legally responsible. Each valid takedown is one strike, and a third strike while two are still active closes the account. You can appeal any strike.

Settings and privacy

⚙

What you can change

Theme (light, dark or system), accent color, font size and layout; playback options like autoplay next, default volume, remember position, captions on by default and default speed; and accessibility options like high contrast, larger tap targets and reduced motion. Settings are saved on this device and applied on every page right away. The Theme button in the More menu switches theme quickly.

🔒

Your data stays in this browser

Everything on the Settings page is stored only in this browser. Nothing is sent to RawReel. You can clear your watch history, favorites, likes, or all RawReel data on this device, and Reset to defaults puts every setting back while keeping your favorites and history.
